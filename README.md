## `02 / FEATURED PROJECT`

<div align="center">

# 🧠 Resume Job Matcher

### *What if an AI recommendation could explain itself?*

</div>

<table>
<tr>
<td width="55%" valign="top">

Most resume-matching systems stop at a similarity score.

I wanted to explore something more useful:

> **Can semantic matching also reveal where a candidate fits — and where they don't?**

The result is a **three-tier SBERT matching system** that compares resumes with job descriptions and provides automated **skill-gap analysis**.

### The system

```text
RESUME
   │
   ▼
SEMANTIC REPRESENTATION
   │
   ▼
SBERT MATCHING
   │
   ▼
MULTI-TIER SCORE
   │
   ▼
SKILL GAP ANALYSIS
   │
   ▼
EXPLAINABLE RESULT
```

</td>

<td width="45%" valign="top">

### `STACK`

`Python`

`SBERT`

`PyTorch`

`Scikit-learn`

`Streamlit`

<br>

### `WHY IT MATTERS`

**Semantic understanding**

Not just keyword matching.

**Transparency**

More context than a single score.

**Practicality**

Deployed as an interactive application.

## `08 / BEYOND THE CODE`

<details>
<summary><b>What I care about</b></summary>

<br>

I enjoy technology most when there is a **real problem behind it**.

I'm interested in systems that don't just demonstrate a technical concept, but actually make information easier to use, decisions easier to make, or processes easier to understand.

That's also why explainability interests me.

For me, good AI isn't only:

`"The model is accurate."`

It's also:

`"We understand what it is doing, and we can use the result responsibly."`

</details>

<br>

<details>
<summary><b>How I approach projects</b></summary>

<br>

```text
1. Understand the problem
          ↓
2. Find and understand the data
          ↓
3. Experiment
          ↓
4. Build the model / system
          ↓
5. Evaluate
          ↓
6. Make it usable
          ↓
7. Iterate
```

I like learning by **building**, testing ideas and improving them as I go.

</details>
